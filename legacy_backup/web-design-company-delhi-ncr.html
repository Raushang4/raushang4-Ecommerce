Why NCR Sites Underperform

What's wrong with most
NCR business websites

We audit a lot of websites in Delhi, Gurgaon, and Noida. The failure modes are consistent — not bad luck, just a local market used to buying websites by the page instead of by the outcome.

01
Bought by the page, not the outcome
Priced as "₹25,000 for a 5-page website." Scoped by how many templates you need, not what the site has to achieve. The output is a brochure that nobody reads and no one enquires from.
02
Looks like every other NCR competitor
Same hero pattern, same service-grid layout, same stock photography, same "about us" page. If three of your competitors have this site, so does the buyer — and nothing is landing with them.
03
Painfully slow on Indian mobile networks
Cheap shared hosting, uncompressed images, twenty plugins, no CDN. On a 4G phone in Gurgaon traffic, the home page takes six seconds. Half your traffic leaves before the fold finishes loading.
04
No CTA structure worth the name
Contact form hidden on a separate page. No phone number in the header. No sticky CTA on mobile. The site treats enquiries like an afterthought instead of the whole point of the build.
05
Built, launched, abandoned
The original freelancer is no longer answering WhatsApp. Logins are with someone who left. Updates stopped eighteen months ago. Maintenance was never in the scope, so it never happened.
06
Aesthetic over architecture
Visual design carried the entire brief. No one thought about information architecture, content modeling, SEO structure, or analytics. The site looks fine in a screenshot and does nothing in the wild.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Web Impetus a web design company in Delhi NCR?
Yes. We are a web design and development studio based in Noida, working with businesses across Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad. Being local means in-person discovery, design reviews, and handoff at your office are part of the engagement when useful.
Do you only work with businesses in Delhi NCR?
No. We work with clients across India and internationally — but being based in Noida means NCR businesses get the option of in-person meetings. For a more general view of the service and process, see our core web design and build page.
What types of businesses in NCR do you design websites for?
Manufacturers in the Noida-Greater Noida industrial belt, B2B service firms in Gurgaon and Delhi, professional services and training institutes across NCR, and D2C brands that need a proper site alongside their store. If your customers are decision-makers, not walk-in retail, we are usually a fit.
Which platforms do you build websites on?
WordPress for most content-driven business sites, Framer for design-forward marketing sites, Webflow for visual-CMS builds, and Shopify or WooCommerce for ecommerce. We recommend the platform based on your team, content needs, and long-term maintenance — not on what is easiest to sell.
Do you design and develop in-house, or do you outsource?
In-house. Design happens in Figma with our team, development happens with our team, and accountability stays in one place. No subcontracted design shops, no offshored build teams you cannot reach. The single-team handoff is the part most agencies get wrong.
How long does a web design project in NCR typically take?
Most builds run four to eight weeks end-to-end. Longer for complex sites, migrations, or multi-phase rollouts. We do not ship two-week websites — we design the structure and content model first so the site actually performs after launch.
Can you redesign an existing website?
Yes. Website redesigns are a large share of what we do. We audit the current site, map what is working, plan redirects to preserve SEO, and rebuild structure, messaging, design, and performance. Usually a redesign for us also means a migration to the right platform.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Yes. Bug fixes, iteration on what the data shows, CMS training for your marketing team, new pages as you grow, and ongoing technical help. The exact model depends on the engagement. For NCR clients, that support can include in-person working sessions when useful.
