What we find

Six places your revenue
escapes unnoticed

Every store leaks revenue in predictable places. Most owners don't see it because they're looking at the wrong numbers. This is what we diagnose on every engagement.

01
A hero that doesn't hook
Your first fold is doing generic work — stock photography, vague taglines, buried CTAs. Visitors make the stay-or-leave call in under 4 seconds.
Most visitors decide to stay or leave before scrolling past the fold
02
Product pages that describe, not sell
Feature lists without benefits. No social proof at the decision point. No answer to "why buy this, why here, why now."
Industry avg product page CVR: 1.2%
03
Cart abandonment above 70%
Surprise shipping costs, no trust signals at checkout, too many required fields. The last step of the funnel is where most revenue dies.
Global avg cart abandonment: 70.2%
04
Ads and landing pages saying different things
Message mismatch between ad creative and landing page kills qualified traffic. If the ad promised a deal and the page talks brand story, they leave.
Message mismatch is one of the most common causes of qualified traffic not converting
05
Mobile that breaks where it counts
60%+ of your traffic is on mobile. Misaligned CTAs, slow load times, and broken tap targets are silently destroying mobile CVR.
Mobile CVR runs 40% below desktop avg
06
The sale ends at checkout
No upsell, no cross-sell, no review prompt, no referral trigger. Acquisition gets all the attention while LTV sits on the table.
Repeat buyers spend 3× more on average

FAQ

Common questions

How much traffic do I need before CRO is worth it?
We recommend a minimum of 1,000 monthly sessions to run statistically valid A/B tests in a reasonable timeframe. Below that, tests take too long to reach significance. That said, our audit and heuristic analysis are valuable at any traffic level — many structural fixes don't require testing to implement.
How long before I see results?
Quick wins from the audit (checkout trust signals, CTA fixes, mobile layout) can be implemented in weeks 1–3 and show impact immediately. Full A/B test cycles typically take 3–6 weeks per test to reach significance. Most clients see measurable CVR improvement within the first 60 days.
Do you work on Shopify only?
No — we work across Shopify, WooCommerce, Webflow, and custom-built sites. The CRO methodology is platform-agnostic; what changes is how we implement test variants. Shopify is our most common platform, so our speed is highest there, but we're comfortable with any major stack.
What makes this different from a one-time UX redesign?
A redesign is a bet. CRO is a process. We run controlled experiments so we know exactly which changes drove which results — and by how much. A redesign might improve things, break things, or do nothing, and you won't know which for months. Our approach gives you a continuous improvement loop backed by data, not opinions.
Is there a minimum engagement period?
Our standard model is a 90-day sprint. This gives us enough time to complete the audit, run 3–5 meaningful tests, implement the winners, and show compounding lift. We don't do month-to-month retainers for CRO — the foundational work in the first 90 days requires commitment from both sides to do it right.
