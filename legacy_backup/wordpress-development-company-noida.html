What NCR Businesses Hit

The usual state of a
WordPress site in NCR

Most of the WordPress sites we audit in Noida, Delhi, and Gurgaon have the same six problems. Not bad luck — just builds shipped by whoever was cheapest, with nobody left around to fix the debt.

01
Freelancer launched and vanished
The original developer is no longer reachable. Admin access is shared. Hosting, domain, and WordPress logins live in three different inboxes. Nobody is accountable for the site's condition today.
02
Page builder bloat killing speed
Elementor Pro, a visual builder theme, twenty plugins, and a shared host in India. Pages take five seconds to load on a 4G phone. Bounce is high, time-on-page is low, and none of it is the content's fault.
03
The CMS is scary to touch
Editing a paragraph on the home page is a five-step ordeal. Your marketing person refuses to update content because they broke the layout last time. WordPress should be the easiest CMS to run, not the riskiest.
04
SEO bolted on, not built in
Yoast or Rank Math is installed. No schema, no internal linking logic, no sitemap discipline, no content architecture. The site ranks for your brand name and nothing else — which is the baseline, not the goal.
05
Security hygiene doesn't exist
No WAF, no backup strategy, admin users unremoved, plugins three versions behind, outdated PHP. Every WordPress site in NCR we audit has at least three of these. One compromise away from a problem.
06
Built once, never maintained
Delivered two years ago, nothing touched since. No update log, no plugin review, no content refresh, no analytics check. Websites compound value when maintained, and rot fast when they aren't.

WordPress Performance Standards

Fast WordPress isn't a plugin.
It's a build discipline.

Most slow WordPress sites in NCR are slow because of stack decisions, not hosting. We set performance targets up front — Lighthouse, LCP, CLS, INP — and the build is measured against them before launch.

90+
Lighthouse Performance Score
Our target for every build. Measured after launch on real devices, not just lab conditions. Slow scores aren't shipped.
<2.5s
Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)
Google's primary measure of perceived load speed. We treat it as a design constraint — image sizing, lazy loading, and asset delivery are all part of the brief.
AA
WCAG 2.1 Accessibility Standard
Contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, ARIA labels — built in from the start. Accessibility is part of the build, not an audit we run at the end.
CLS < 0.1 — no layout shift
INP < 200ms — instant interactions
