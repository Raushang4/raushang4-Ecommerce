## 2024-05-24 - Custom Modal Dialog Accessibility\n**Learning:** When implementing custom modals without the standard HTML `<dialog>` element, they lack built-in screen reader announcements and focus trapping/management. This can make them inaccessible or confusing to keyboard and screen reader users.\n**Action:** Always ensure custom modals have `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, appropriate `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby` attributes pointing to dynamic IDs, an `Escape` key listener (with cleanup on unmount), and initial focus management (auto-focusing the primary action element upon opening).
